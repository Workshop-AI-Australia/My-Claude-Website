Simpro and AI

Using AI with Simpro

Yes, AI can work with your Simpro data. Simpro has an open API, its own AI in the Lightning platform, and third-party connectors that let tools such as Claude and ChatGPT read it, and sometimes write to it. The right route depends on what you want to ask, what you want automated and what you want to spend. Here's a plain guide from a Melbourne team that has built on Simpro.

Written by Justin Watts, Director of Workshop AI, Hobsons Bay, Melbourne · Last reviewed 15 October 2026
General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Three routesHow AI connects to Simpro

1. Simpro's own AI

Simpro launched Lightning in May 2026, describing it as an AI-native operating platform. Its AI, called Cooper, comes with four agents (FieldReady, JobReady, JobScribe and JobBrief), and JustAsk answers plain-English questions about things like technician margins, overdue invoices and customer history. A July 2026 release added an AI scheduler and expanded JustAsk. At launch, Lightning was priced as an extra 15 per cent on existing contracts, rising to 25 per cent after 31 May 2026.

Good for: questions and tasks inside Simpro, with nothing to connect. Limit: it sees Simpro, not your inbox, your accounts or your shared drive.

2. Ready-made connectors

Zapier connects to Simpro with triggers such as new or updated jobs and quotes, and actions such as creating a service job or quote, and Simpro charges nothing for the Zapier connection. Several services also offer MCP connectors, the standard way AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT use other software: Zapier MCP, viaSocket and Tugger, a read-only option listed on the Simpro Marketplace.

Good for: quick wins and simple automations. Limit: you're bound by what each connector exposes, and some connect with write access by default.

3. A custom build on the API

Simpro's REST API can view, create and modify records across customers, sites, jobs, cost centres, recurring jobs, quotes, invoices, schedules, stock, purchase orders and assets, and it can send webhooks when a job changes stage. Simpro's Australian pricing page lists Open API on the base plan. Simpro doesn't offer one-on-one API support, and points people to its forum or a partner.

Good for: joining Simpro to email, Xero and documents, branded reporting, and agents that do a defined job. Limit: it needs building and looking after.

Once it's connectedQuestions worth asking your Simpro data

  • "What's sitting in Complete but not yet invoiced, and for how long?" Simpro's job stages run Pending, Progress, Complete, Invoiced and Archived, and Complete means the work is done and waiting to be invoiced.
  • "Which quotes in Open or Progress haven't been followed up in a fortnight?" (Quote stages run Open, Progress, Complete, Approved and Closed or Archived.)
  • "Which recurring jobs fall due next month, and which maintenance customers don't have one set up?"
  • "Which cost centres on this project are running over?"
  • "Draft this month's maintenance report for this customer, in our branding."

Start safelyRead-only first, then earn the right to write

  • Start read-only. Let AI look before it's allowed to change anything. Add write access one action at a time, with a person approving.
  • Treat API keys like master keys. Simpro's developer documentation describes an API key as a single access token that never expires. Give each integration its own credentials, limit who holds them, and remove them when they're no longer needed.
  • Mind the limits. The API returns at most 250 records per request and rejects bursts above 10 requests a second to one build, so well-built integrations page through data patiently.
  • Keep a person on the trigger. Anything sent to a customer, any price and any job created should have a person approving it until the results have earned trust.

5 questionsQuestions we hear

Can Claude or ChatGPT connect directly to Simpro?

Yes, through a connector. Third-party MCP connectors from services such as Zapier, viaSocket and Tugger let Claude and ChatGPT read Simpro data, and some can also create or update records. A custom connection built on Simpro's API gives you full control over what the AI can see and do.

Do we need Simpro Lightning to use AI?

No. Lightning is Simpro's own route, built in and priced as a percentage on top of your contract. Connectors and custom builds work through Simpro's API instead. Many businesses will use both: Simpro's AI inside Simpro, and a connected assistant for work that spans email, accounts and documents.

Is there an official Simpro MCP server?

We haven't found one from Simpro itself as of October 2026. The MCP connectors available come from third parties. Check what each one can read and write before you connect it.

Can AI create jobs and quotes in Simpro?

Yes. The API and connectors such as Zapier can create jobs and quotes. Start with AI preparing them for a person to approve, and only let it act on its own once you trust the results.

What does it cost to connect AI to Simpro?

It depends on the route. Simpro's Lightning is priced as a percentage of your Simpro contract, ready-made connectors charge their own subscriptions or usage fees, and a custom build costs what it takes to build and run.
