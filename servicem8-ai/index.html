ServiceM8 and AI

Using AI with ServiceM8

Yes, AI can work with your ServiceM8 data, and ServiceM8 makes it easier than most. It has AI built in, its own connector that plugs straight into Claude and ChatGPT, and an open API for anything custom. The catch is that the connector can do more than read, including sending messages to your customers, so it needs setting up with care. Here's a plain guide from a Melbourne team that sets up AI for trade businesses.

Three routesHow AI connects to ServiceM8

1. ServiceM8's own AI

ServiceM8 has AI built into the app: a writing helper for emails, texts, quotes and invoice descriptions, AI quoting and an AI invoice generator. Its September 2025 update added an Office Agent that handles routine admin such as chasing quotes, drafting replies and flagging overdue tasks, with you approving or declining what it suggests. The same update added a Phone Agent that answers business calls, and a Smart Report Helper that builds a custom report from a plain-English description.

Good for: writing, quoting, calls and reports inside ServiceM8, with nothing to connect. Limit: it sees ServiceM8, not your inbox, your accounts or your shared drive.

2. ServiceM8's own connector

ServiceM8 runs its own MCP server, the standard way AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT use other software. You add it as a connector at api.servicem8.com/mcp and sign in to ServiceM8, with no developer needed. It can search clients and jobs, read job details, diary notes and staff availability, and it can also create jobs from templates, schedule them, change job status and send job-linked emails and SMS. Third-party connectors also exist, including Zapier MCP and viaSocket.

Good for: asking questions and doing quick jobs from the AI tool you already use. Limit: the same connector can message your customers, so decide what it's allowed to do before you switch it on.

3. A custom build on the API

ServiceM8's REST API uses an API key, created under Settings, then API Keys, for a business's own integrations, and OAuth for add-ons sold to other businesses. It allows 180 requests a minute and 20,000 a day, so integrations that sync a lot of history need to pace themselves.

Good for: joining ServiceM8 to email, Xero and documents, and agents that run on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. Limit: it needs building and looking after.

Once it's connectedQuestions worth asking your ServiceM8 data

ServiceM8's four job statuses are Quote, Work Order, Completed and Unsuccessful, and Completed means the work is finished and the job is ready to invoice. The statuses can't be customised, so ServiceM8 uses queues for jobs that are waiting on something.

  • "Which jobs are Completed but not yet invoiced, and for how long?"
  • "Which jobs are still at Quote after a fortnight, and who were they for?"
  • "Which jobs have been sitting in a queue the longest, and what are they waiting on?"
  • "What did we mark Unsuccessful last quarter, and is there a pattern in why?"
  • "Draft a follow-up email for each quote older than 14 days, for me to check before anything is sent."

Start safelyDecide what it may do before you connect it

  • Know what the connector can change. ServiceM8's connector can change job status and send emails and SMS to customers. Your AI tool may ask before running these, depending on its settings, so check them before the first real request.
  • On business plans, an owner goes first. On Claude's Team and Enterprise plans, an Owner adds ServiceM8 for the organisation before staff can connect their own accounts.
  • Treat API keys like master keys. A private integration connects straight to your account with an API key. Give each integration its own key, limit who holds them, and remove them when they're no longer needed.
  • Mind the limits. Past 180 requests a minute or 20,000 a day, the API refuses requests, so well-built integrations pace themselves.
  • Keep a person on the trigger. Anything sent to a customer, any price and any job created should have a person approving it until the results have earned trust.

5 questionsQuestions we hear

Can Claude or ChatGPT connect directly to ServiceM8?

Yes. ServiceM8 runs its own MCP connector, which you add to Claude as a custom connector or to ChatGPT, then sign in with your ServiceM8 account. Third-party connectors from Zapier and viaSocket also work.

Is there an official ServiceM8 MCP server?

Yes. ServiceM8 hosts its own, and announced it in its September 2025 update. A separate developer MCP server helps developers who are building ServiceM8 integrations search its API and documentation.

Can AI send emails and texts to our customers from ServiceM8?

Yes, which is why it needs care. The official connector can compose and send job-linked emails and SMS. Start with AI drafting and a person sending, and only let it send on its own once you trust the results.

Do we still need ServiceM8's own AI if we connect Claude or ChatGPT?

They do different jobs. ServiceM8's AI works inside ServiceM8, on its screens and its phone line. A connected assistant works across ServiceM8, your email, accounts and documents. Many businesses will use both.

What does it cost to connect AI to ServiceM8?

It depends on the route. ServiceM8's own connector needs no developer, though your AI tool's plan has to allow connectors. Third-party connectors charge their own subscriptions or usage fees, and a custom build costs what it takes to build and run.
