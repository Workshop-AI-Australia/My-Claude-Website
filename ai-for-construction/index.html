AI for builders and construction companies

For builders and construction companies, AI is best at the document load: finding what was agreed across job folders, drafting variations, RFIs and site reports, keeping SWMS current, and getting payment claims and schedules out on time. Site decisions, prices and sign-offs stay with your people.

Written by Justin Watts, Director of Workshop AI, Hobsons Bay, Melbourne · Last reviewed 15 October 2026
General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Where the time goesFive document jobs AI handles well in construction

  • Finding what was agreed. Drawings, RFIs, variations, contracts and emails sit across folders and inboxes. AI can answer "what did we agree about the window change on that job?" and point to the document it came from.
  • Variations. For domestic work in Victoria, a variation generally has to be agreed in writing on a variation notice, with the new price and completion date, before the work is done. AI drafts the notice from site notes, and a register shows which variations are still waiting on a price or a signature before anyone starts.
  • Payment claims and schedules. Whether you're claiming or receiving claims, the security of payment timeframes are tight. AI can draft claims in the required form and list every claim you've received with the date its payment schedule is due.
  • Site diaries, RFIs and defects. A supervisor's voice note and photos become a diary entry, an RFI or a defect item, filed against the job. Defect lists are tracked to close-out, which often decides when you get paid.
  • Tenders. AI reads tender documents for scope gaps, special conditions, insurance requirements and deadlines, so they're in front of you when you price.

The rules AI helps you keepVariations, payment claims and SWMS

Security of payment

Victoria's security of payment scheme covers most construction contracts, including subcontracts, but not a builder's contract with a home owner. A payment claim must identify the work, state the amount and say it's made under the Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 2002. Since 15 April 2026, claims are monthly, served from the last day of the month, and can be made for up to six months after practical completion.

If you receive a claim, a payment schedule is due within 10 business days, or you become liable for the full amount. An AI register of incoming claims with their deadlines is cheap insurance.

Domestic building contracts

Changes to Victoria's domestic building contract laws, including a single variation process, were passed in 2025, with the start date set by the government. AI-drafted templates are easy to update when the rules change, provided someone owns that job. Check Consumer Affairs Victoria for when the new rules apply.

SWMS

Under Victoria's OHS Regulations, a Safe Work Method Statement must be prepared before high-risk construction work starts, such as work with a risk of falling more than two metres, trenches deeper than 1.5 metres, demolition or work involving asbestos. It must be kept for the duration of the work and reviewed when the work changes or after an incident.

AI can draft a SWMS from your templates and the job details, and flag when a change on site means one needs reviewing. The hazard assessment, and the consultation with the workers involved, stay with your people.

A sensible first projectAsk the job

Pick one live project. Give AI read-only access to its folders and the project inbox. Your supervisors and office team can then ask questions in plain English, such as "what's outstanding from the last site meeting?" or "which variations aren't signed yet?", and get answers that link to the source document.

It works best when job files are organised consistently, and setting that up is often half the value. Once one project works, the same setup rolls out to the next.

5 questionsQuestions we hear

Can AI write a variation notice?

It can draft one from site notes in seconds, with the description, price and change to the completion date. For domestic work the variation generally has to be agreed in writing before the work is done, so the builder checks the draft and the owner signs it first.

Can AI prepare our security of payment claims?

Yes, as drafts. AI can prepare each month's claim from your job data with the required wording and keep a register of claims you receive, with the 10-business-day deadline for each payment schedule. Check every claim and schedule before it's served. This is general information, not legal advice.

Can AI keep our SWMS up to date when the work changes?

It can flag when it should happen. If site notes or a variation show the work has changed, AI can mark the affected SWMS for review and draft the changes. WorkSafe Victoria requires a SWMS to be reviewed when the work changes, and the review itself needs the people who know the site.

Can AI find things across our job folders and emails?

Yes, and it's often the biggest win for builders. With read-only access to a project's folders and inbox, AI can answer questions and link to the drawing, RFI or email the answer came from. It works much better when files are named and filed consistently.
