AI for business: put it to work

A hands-on, two-hour workshop for your business program. Owners bring a laptop, set AI up safely for their own business, and leave with it working.

Ask for the outline and feeBook a 20-minute chat

Run by Justin Watts and Kylie, Workshop AI, Hobsons Bay, Melbourne · Updated 2 November 2026

The workshopPractical, not a tour of tools

Many business programs have already run an introduction to AI. This is the practical next step, and it works just as well on its own. Everyone works on their own business, with examples from trades, building and service businesses.

  • Set AI up safely: what never to paste in, the settings to change, how to spot AI scams, and a one-page AI policy.
  • Set it up once: a saved project that already knows the business, its documents and its tone, so the answers stay consistent.
  • Connect it: to the email, calendar or files people already use, where their plan allows.

Who it's forBusy owners and small teams

  • Owners and small teams in trades, building and service businesses
  • Hospitality, health and retail owners who spend their evenings on admin
  • Anyone who has tried AI and wants it to know their business
  • No technical background needed

OutcomesEveryone leaves with

  • Their own AI set-upSaved and ready to use in their business the next morning.
  • A one-page AI policyWritten for their business, so the whole team uses AI the same safe way.
  • A take-home guideThey can load it into their own AI tool and keep asking it questions.

Title

AI for business: put it to work

Alternative title

Short on time? Put AI to work on your admin

Description

Bring your laptop and leave with AI working for your business. In two hands-on hours you'll set AI up safely, save a set-up that already knows your business, and connect it to the email, calendar or files you already use. Examples come from trades, building and service businesses. No technical background needed.

Learning outcome

At the end of this workshop, participants will be able to write a one-page AI policy, set up an AI assistant that knows their business, and connect it to a tool they already use to cut admin time.

PresentersTwo of us in the room

Justin Watts, Director

Justin has spent more than 15 years building the systems growing businesses run on, and the last few applying AI across them. At Workshop AI he helps trade and service businesses hand their repetitive office work to AI: quotes, double entry and the same questions answered over and over.

Kylie, Co-founder

Kylie leads client delivery at Workshop AI and co-presents, so attendees get hands-on help at their laptops rather than watching from the back of the room.

Workshop AI clients include Mattick Air, a Melbourne commercial air-conditioning business, and Rice Group, a regional NSW builder. For Mattick Air, we connected its job management system so the office can ask it questions in plain English. More on who we are on the company facts page.

Our approachA teaching session, not a sales pitch

We don't promote our services during the session. We share our contact details at the close and never use registration lists for marketing.
