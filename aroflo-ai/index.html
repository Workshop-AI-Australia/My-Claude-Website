AroFlo and AI

Using AI with AroFlo

Yes, AI can work with your AroFlo data. AroFlo is part of Simpro Group and now shares its AI, called Cooper, through AroFlo Lightning. It also has an open API, though its daily limits shape what you can build on it. Here's a plain guide from a Melbourne team that has built on Simpro Group's software.

Written by Justin Watts, Director of Workshop AI, Hobsons Bay, Melbourne · Last reviewed 20 November 2026
General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Three routesHow AI connects to AroFlo

1. AroFlo's own AI: Lightning

AroFlo has been part of Simpro Group since November 2021. In May 2026 Simpro Group launched Lightning across its products, including AroFlo Lightning in Australia and New Zealand, with an AI called Cooper at the centre. JustAsk answers plain-English questions about your business, such as which jobs are running over on labour or who owes you money.

AroFlo's pricing page lists JustAsk, FieldReady, JobReady, JobScribe and JobBrief among the AI features all plans include. AroFlo also added AI Rewrite in February 2026, which can write an invoice description from the task. Its RAIN release lists Smart Import, which brings work orders and quote requests from emails and portals into AroFlo, though AroFlo says the timing may shift. IT Brief reported in November 2026 that Simpro Group's JustDo office agent, which drafts jobs and quotes for a person to approve, is rolling out to AroFlo Lightning customers too.

Good for: questions and tasks inside AroFlo, with nothing to connect. Limit: it sees AroFlo, not your inbox, your accounts or your shared drive.

2. Ready-made connectors

AroFlo works with Zapier, for example to update a task's details and status from a mobile form. We haven't found an official AroFlo MCP connector for Claude or ChatGPT as of November 2026, though we've seen at least one small community-built one.

Good for: simple automations between AroFlo and other apps. Limit: you're bound by what each connector exposes.

3. A custom build on the API

AroFlo's API uses HMAC-SHA512 signed requests and returns XML by default, or JSON on request. Its limits are tight: 1 request a second, 60 a minute and 2,000 a day. AroFlo doesn't offer coding support for the API, so you'll need a developer or a partner.

Good for: joining AroFlo to email, Xero and documents, branded reporting, and agents that do a defined job. Limit: it needs building around the daily limit, and looking after.

Once it's connectedQuestions worth asking your AroFlo data

In AroFlo, jobs are tasks. Setting a task to Completed means the work is done and it's ready for invoicing or archiving, and the Completed Tasks list shows closed tasks waiting to be invoiced. Quotes carry their own status and acceptance status.

  • "Which tasks are Completed but not yet invoiced, and for how long?"
  • "Which approved quotes still have no acceptance recorded after a fortnight?"
  • "Which work orders came in by email this week that aren't in AroFlo yet?"
  • "Which tasks are running over on labour this month?"
  • "Draft this month's maintenance summary for this customer, in our branding."

Start safelyRead-only first, and design for the daily limit

  • Start read-only. Let AI look before it's allowed to change anything. Add write access one action at a time, with a person approving.
  • Guard the secret key. Generating a new key means updating every integration that uses it, so share it with as few people as possible and keep a record of where it's used.
  • Design for 2,000 calls a day. At 2,000 requests a day, a build can't ask AroFlo live every time someone has a question. Sync changes on a schedule, work from a copy, and spread large history pulls out.
  • Keep a person on the trigger. Anything sent to a customer, any price and any task created should have a person approving it until the results have earned trust.

5 questionsQuestions we hear

Is AroFlo the same as Simpro now?

No. Both belong to Simpro Group, which said at the time of the purchase that the leadership teams would operate independently, and they remain separate products with separate APIs. Since May 2026 they share the same AI layer, Lightning, and the same AI, Cooper. If you're comparing, see using AI with Simpro.

Can Claude or ChatGPT connect directly to AroFlo?

Not through an official connector that we've found as of November 2026. A custom connection built on AroFlo's API gives you control over what the AI can see and do, within AroFlo's daily request limit.

Do we need AroFlo Lightning to use AI?

No. Lightning is AroFlo's own route, and its pricing page lists JustAsk and several agents among the AI features in all plans. Connectors and custom builds work through the API instead. Many businesses will use both: AroFlo's AI inside AroFlo, and a connected assistant for work that spans email, accounts and documents.

Is 2,000 API calls a day enough?

It can be, if the build syncs changes on a schedule and works from a copy rather than calling AroFlo every time someone asks a question. Pulling years of history at once may need spreading over several days. The limits also cap requests at one a second.

What does it cost to connect AI to AroFlo?

It depends on the route. AroFlo lists its Lightning features in its plans, ready-made connectors charge their own subscriptions or usage fees, and a custom build costs what it takes to build and run.
