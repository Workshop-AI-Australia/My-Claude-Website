  5. RF-05

    AI where it doesn’t belong

    “An AI consultant will find a use for AI in everything. And is it safe?”

    Traces to your adAssess proposed AI solutions and recommend appropriate approaches, including automation, integration or system development.
    Not runPass
    My answer

    Every agent idea gets four questions. Is the input structured? Are the steps definable? Is the output checkable? What does a wrong answer cost? A clear no on any of the first three and I don’t build it. A logistics client got a pipeline with no AI at run time.

    How you’d test it
    Given
    any agent I propose for your client.
    When
    you look at what it can touch.
    Then
    access is read-only by default and scoped, with a handover note on how to stop it.
  6. RF-06

    No Workato

    “Integration platforms are highly regarded. He hasn’t used Workato.”

    Traces to your adExperience with Model Context Protocol (MCP), AI integrations or connectors. Exposure to Workato or similar integration platforms.
    Not runPass
    My answer

    I haven’t used Workato, and I won’t claim it. The integrations I build are in code. I built the MCP connector that brings Mattick Air’s Simpro data into Claude, and a logistics client’s manifests now flow to dockets and billing. I’ve built integrations and connectors, just not on Workato.

    How you’d test it
    Given
    the MCP and connectors line in your ad.
    When
    you ask me about the Mattick Air connector.
    Then
    I can show you what it does and how the team uses it.
