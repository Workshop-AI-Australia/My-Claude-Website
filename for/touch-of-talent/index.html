  1. Brief 01OpenSigned off

    A full-time role?

    The concern

    “He runs his own consultancy. Will he really be full time?”

    My answer

    Fair question. Someone who runs their own consultancy probably isn’t who you pictured for a full-time role. But this job reads a lot like my week, and I’m keen to do it properly. The role would have my full attention.

    How you’d measure it

    Whether my work shows it, build after build.

  2. Brief 02OpenSigned off

    Not on your platforms

    The concern

    “The ad lists Copilot Studio, Power Automate, n8n, Make and Zapier. He builds in Claude.”

    My answer

    I’ve used n8n, Make and Zapier hands-on. I build in Claude and Claude Code, with MCP connectors to join systems up, including one that brings Mattick Air’s Simpro data into Claude. Power Automate and Copilot Studio are my honest gap, and an easy one to close.

    How you’d measure it

    The first workflow I build on your platform, judged against a measure we agree before I build it.

  3. Brief 03OpenSigned off

    Small clients, several teams

    The concern

    “His clients are small businesses. We have several teams.”

    My answer

    True, my clients are small. My scale comes from Ailo, where I led the company-wide Salesforce to HubSpot move across sales, marketing and service. I also built an enablement platform there that people kept using. It had 83% monthly use across its 19 users, none required to.

    How you’d measure it

    Monthly use team by team, so a team that hasn’t taken it up shows early.

  4. Brief 04OpenSigned off

    Not an agency person

    The concern

    “He’s never worked in an agency.”

    My answer

    Not agency-side. At Ailo I owned about $150,000 a year of sales and marketing software. My marketing work there included an AI engine that makes four branded social tiles per user, and a campaign that reached 4,775 users, 855% of target, for $3,263. A marketing team’s day is familiar ground.

    How you’d measure it

    Whether your teams start bringing me their own problems to fix.

  5. Brief 05OpenSigned off

    AI in everything?

    The concern

    “Will he push AI into everything? And is our clients’ data safe?”

    My answer

    Every build gets four questions. Is the input structured? Are the steps definable? Is the output checkable? What does a wrong answer cost? A clear no on any of the first three and I don’t build it. Access is scoped and read-only; client data stays out of code and logs.

    How you’d measure it

    For any build: a written list of what it can access, and no client data in its code or logs.

  6. Brief 06OpenSigned off

    How will we know it worked?

    The concern

    “How will we know any of this worked?”

    My answer

    Success measures are agreed before each build. At Mattick Air, after rollout, I tracked logins and connected tools person by person and reported uptake to the owner, with what to fix next. Here, I’d track adoption the same way and report the impact to your leadership.

    How you’d measure it

    The measure we agree before each build, and the report against it afterwards.

  7. Brief 07

    Not a red flag

    The extra in the job bag: what comes with me.

    What comes with me

    I’d start with the internal champions your ad mentions: people already curious, each fixing one real problem in their own work. Workshops ready to run, including “Agents: what to build and what to leave alone”. A handover note with every release. What I build is yours to run.
