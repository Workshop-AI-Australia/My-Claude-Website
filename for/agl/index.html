The doubts I'd have in your seat, scored the way I'd score an AI opportunity, each with its mitigation. Tap a flag to see where it lands. Don't skip the last row.

How it's scored

Likelihood times impact, before and after.

Likelihood is how likely the doubt turns out to be right. Impact is what it would cost AGL if it did. Both are scored 1 to 5 and multiplied. Each flag shows where it starts, then where it lands after mitigation.

05Will it stick?12 High→6 Medium

"Training is easy to run. Will anyone still be using it in month six?"

Fair worry. At Mattick Air the group session didn't change how most people worked, so I switched to one-to-ones on each person's own work and tracked uptake person by person. At Ailo, an enablement platform I built averaged 83% monthly use across 19 users, none required to.

MitigationI track uptake person by person, switch to one-to-ones on people's own work where group sessions don't land, and keep training until the tools are theirs.

Inherent12 HighL4 × I3→Residual6 MediumL2 × I3

Medium. Adoption needs ongoing follow-up, so I keep tracking it.

06Safe in a business like ours?10 High→4 Low

"We can't have AI making calls that nobody checked."

Agreed. Before any agent I ask what a wrong answer costs. Access is read-only by default and scoped: an ongoing construction client's index agent can write to one site only, and client data stays out of code and logs. High or hard to undo: it drafts, a person decides.

MitigationI put four questions to every agent idea, keep access read-only by default, ship every change in a small, tested batch and check usage rules with a quiz. Inside AGL, your own privacy, cyber and risk review comes first.
