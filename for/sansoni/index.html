    1. Red flag 01 of 06CHECKED

      “He’s a consultant. We advertised a job, not an engagement.”

      That’s right, and I wouldn’t take it on as an employee. I’d work with Mark and your team leads through Workshop AI, as a consultant. I’d take this on as a Workshop AI engagement. The work in your ad is the work I do for clients.

      How to check itSection 03 of my capability statement, “A Workshop AI engagement”.

    2. Red flag 02 of 06CHECKED

      “Rollouts fade after the launch session.”

      Mine did once, and my half page tells that story. Now I’d book the one-to-ones first and watch usage from the start. At Ailo, the enablement platform I built reached 83% monthly use across 19 users.

      How to check itMy half page, and section 01 of my capability statement, under Measuring results.

    3. Red flag 03 of 06CHECKED

      “Can he build, or does he only train?”

      Both. For a construction group, the owner had built a first check with Claude. I turned it into a nightly accounts payable agent that runs in production. A second agent, which indexes their SharePoint, is built and in handover.

      How to check itThe spotlight in my capability statement: an owner’s Claude prototype, turned into a nightly team process.

    4. Red flag 04 of 06CHECKED

      “Will he push AI into everything?”

      No. Every idea passes four questions before I build, and a clear no on any of the first three means I don’t build it. For a logistics business, the right answer was plain automation with no AI at run time, and it’s live.

      How to check itPage 2 of my capability statement lists the four questions. Ask me which ideas failed them.

    5. Red flag 05 of 06CHECKED

      “Is our member data safe?”

      I follow Australia’s Guidance for AI Adoption. Usage rules are agreed up front and checked with a quiz, and access is read-only by default. Anything hard to undo stays with a person. I’d also agree with you which tasks should stay human-led.

      How to check itSection 01 of my capability statement, under Keeping AI use safe.

    6. Red flag 06 of 06CHECKED

      “He’s never worked in a coaching business, or a group this size.”

      True. A coaching business would be new to me, so I’d start with your team leads, one team at a time. At Ailo I led teams of up to ten and led the Salesforce to HubSpot move. I owned a sales and marketing software budget of about $150k a year.

      How to check itAsk me to walk you through the Salesforce to HubSpot move, from the first conversation to who used it.

    7. Bonus card · Bracketed targets

      “Our targets are still in brackets.”

      Your ad leaves the targets in brackets until the baseline is in, which is how I’d want it. I’d read the baseline before agreeing any number. I track logins and connected tools person by person, and count time saved as minutes per task times how often.

      How to check itSection 03 of my capability statement, under A usage baseline first.
