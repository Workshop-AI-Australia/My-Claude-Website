    1. Red flag 01 of 06ANSWERED

      “We advertised for a consultant. He’s bringing a consultancy.”

      You’d get one person in the room with your clients: me. I’d run the discovery, shape proposals with your sales team, and stay with your engineers through delivery so the build matches what the client needs. One point of contact for your clients, start to finish.

      How to check itAsk me exactly who would do what, and who your clients would deal with.

    2. Red flag 02 of 06ANSWERED

      “Is this a consultancy pitch dressed up as an application?”

      It’s an application from someone who already does this work. A fixed term works for me, and I’d rather show you how I work than pretend to be a standard candidate. If a different shape suits you better, including the role as advertised, I’m open to finding one.

      How to check itBring one real client problem to a first conversation, and watch how I work on it.

    3. Red flag 03 of 06ANSWERED

      “He does what we do. Is he after our clients?”

      Fair question. What Workshop AI does sits close to what Perform Digital does, and I’d rather say that up front than have you find it. In your seat, the work and the client relationships are yours. I’m offering to do the job you advertised, in the open.

      How to check itAsk me how I’d keep your clients and my own practice apart, and put it in writing.

    4. Red flag 04 of 06ANSWERED

      “Workshop AI is brand new. Where are the three years?”

      Mostly at Ailo, a property software company, where I spent nearly twelve years on in-house enablement and client-facing work, training non-technical colleagues to build their own tools. Workshop AI started this year. The years are real; only the business name is new.

      How to check itAsk me to walk one Ailo rollout through, from the first conversation to who still uses it.

    5. Red flag 05 of 06ANSWERED

      “Will he push AI into everything?”

      No. For a logistics business whose agents had started to drift, the right answer was plain automation with no AI at run time. I follow Australia’s Guidance for AI Adoption, which builds on the Voluntary AI Safety Standard. A good readiness assessment says where AI shouldn’t go.

      How to check itAsk me which ideas I’ve talked clients out of building, and why.

    6. Red flag 06 of 06ANSWERED

      “His clients are small businesses, not regulated industries.”

      They are, and financial services, health and government would be new to me. What carries is the method. At a construction group I mapped how an invoice gets paid, then sat beside the finance officer while the agent ran. I stay close until it matches what the client needs.

      How to check itAsk what changed after those first runs, and what I wrote down before building.

    7. Schedule A · What’s included

      Schedule A: what’s included.

      Your engineers build it. I make sure it’s the right thing. What comes with me: four questions before any agent, a governance one-pager, a written list of what will and won’t be built, and an engineer if a job needs extra hands. The method stays with your team.

      How to check itMy capability statement sets out the same shape.
