Induction checklist

For nbn
    Tap the card
    1. Red flag 01 of 06CHECKED

      “We advertised for one person. He’s bringing a business.”

      You’d get one person in the room: me. An engineer sits behind me for the deep build work, so I can stay with your people and good ideas don’t queue behind one person. One point of contact, with capacity that flexes with your roadmap.

      How to verify itAsk me exactly who builds what, and who you’d deal with.

    2. Red flag 02 of 06CHECKED

      “Is this a consultancy pitch dressed up as an application?”

      It’s an application from someone who already does this work. I’d rather show you how I work than pretend to be a standard candidate. If a different shape suits nbn better, including the permanent role as advertised, I’m open to finding one.

      How to verify itBring one real problem from one of your teams to a first conversation, and watch how I work on it.

    3. Red flag 03 of 06CHECKED

      “He’s never used Calyx.”

      True. I haven’t used Calyx. What transfers is the method: sit with the team, map the steps, put each idea through four questions, then check who’s still using it. I’d learn Calyx from the people already running it, and say so when I don’t know.

      How to verify itAsk me to walk one Calyx use case through that method, step by step.

    4. Red flag 04 of 06CHECKED

      “His clients are much smaller than nbn.”

      They are. My clients are small businesses, and working alongside formal engineering and architecture teams at nbn’s scale would be new to me. What carries is the method. I’d start with one team and one workflow they want fixed, then spread what works.

      How to verify itAsk one of my client referees how I started with one team and spread what works.

    5. Red flag 05 of 06CHECKED

      “Will he push AI into everything?”

      No. A clear no on any of my first three questions and I don’t build it, and some ideas get a spreadsheet or a phone call instead. I follow Australia’s Guidance for AI Adoption with clients. Telling people where AI shouldn’t go is part of the job.

      How to verify itAsk me which ideas I’ve talked clients out of building, and why.

    6. Red flag 06 of 06CHECKED

      “Launching is the easy part. Who follows up?”

      That’s the part I watch most. After rollout I track uptake person by person. At a construction group, the first runs showed finance needed to see every row, not just the exceptions, so I changed the design. I report uptake with what to fix next.

      How to verify itAsk what changed after those first runs, and how I’d report uptake to you.

    7. Bonus card · Competencies

      Plus a competencies card.

      Induction cards list what the holder is cleared for. Mine lists what comes with me: an engineer for the deep build work, capacity that flexes with your roadmap, the four questions before any agent, and guides kept in tools your team already uses. The method stays with your team.

      How to verify itSection 03 of my capability statement sets out the same shape.
