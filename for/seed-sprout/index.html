Our guessesThree what ifs we'd guess at.

These come from the outside: your website, your reviews and the press. They're here to show the shape of the thing, not to tell you where to start.

Our guessMarketing and supply chain

“What if we knew which bundles a big week would empty first, before Sophie's next video went live?”

Why we'd guess it: Sophie's posts reach a big audience, and many of your bundles and multibuys share the same products.

  1. SproutA check that links each bundle to its products and shows which would run short first.
  2. Day 3,000It reads live stock and runs before every launch or post, with a heads-up for the team.
Our guessWholesale

“What if a stockist could check stock and reorder without emailing us?”

Why we'd guess it: you have more than 70 stockists joining through an application form, and you've named wholesale as one of your next areas of growth.

  1. SproutA simple reorder page built from your wholesale price list.
  2. Day 3,000Connected to stock and orders, with a nudge when a regular stockist goes quiet.
Our guessCustomer care and product

“What if anyone who lost a lid could find the right spare in seconds?”

Why we'd guess it: lids and seals come up in about one in six of your reviews, and one customer asked for spares outright. Of the three, it also fits day 3,000 best, because it keeps your products in use for longer.

  1. SproutA “which part fits?” finder built from your product range.
  2. Day 3,000In your help centre and updated with every new range. When there's no spare yet, it tells the product team someone asked.

The first stepOne what if, one week.

It starts with a chat. After that it barely touches your team, so it can happen now, in your busiest stretch, without waiting for February.

1

Have a chat

First step

Twenty minutes with you both. We'll hear the what ifs floating around, and about anything the team has built that worked once.

2

Pick one what if together

A seed, or a sprout someone has already grown. We choose the one worth doing first.

3

We spend a week on it

We build it, or pick up what's already there. If it needs access to anything, it starts read-only.

4

You try it, then decide

At the end of the week you'll have something to try. Then it's your call: grow it, change it, or leave it there.
