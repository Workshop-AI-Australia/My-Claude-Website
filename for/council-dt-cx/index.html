    1. Red flag 01 of 06LOGGED

      “He hasn’t worked in local government.”

      I haven’t, and I won’t dress it up. Workshop AI is based in Altona North, so Melbourne’s south‑west is home. I’d start by sitting with the people who do the work, and with what residents are already asking.

      How to check itSection 02 of my capability statement, under Highly regarded, says the same.

    2. Red flag 02 of 06LOGGED

      “He hasn’t run a council website or enterprise systems.”

      Running a council’s digital platforms would be new to me. What I can show is the AI side: I follow Australia’s Guidance for AI Adoption, and I say where AI shouldn’t go. I’ve worked in Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce and HubSpot, and connected agents to SharePoint.

      How to check itSection 02 of my capability statement, under AI, websites and digital platforms.

    3. Red flag 03 of 06LOGGED

      “He runs a consultancy. This is a council coordinator role.”

      It is, and I wouldn’t take it on as an employee. I’d work with the council through Workshop AI, as a consultant. I’d do this as a Workshop AI engagement. A consultancy isn’t the usual shape for a council coordinator, so I’d rather say it plainly.

      How to check itSection 03 of my capability statement, “A Workshop AI engagement”.

    4. Red flag 04 of 06LOGGED

      “His clients are small businesses, not a council.”

      My clients so far have been small businesses. The method still holds. I find the step people still do by hand, fix that one, then look for the next. For a construction group, that became a nightly accounts payable check, now in production.

      How to check itAsk me how the construction group’s nightly check reports every row, right or wrong and why.

    5. Red flag 05 of 06LOGGED

      “Workshop AI only started this year.”

      It did. The years behind it aren’t new: nearly twelve were at Ailo, a property software company, where I led teams of up to ten and taught non-technical colleagues to build their own tools. Only the practice name is new.

      How to check itSection 02 of my capability statement, under Building and leading high performing teams.

    6. Red flag 06 of 06LOGGED

      “Is his customer experience and website experience thin?”

      I don’t have a council website or a formal customer experience program to point to. For anything a resident sees, my default is the one I use with clients: AI drafts, and a person sends. I start with what residents are actually asking for.

      How to check itSection 02 of my capability statement, under Customer experience principles.

    7. Bonus request · 07

      “Where would he start?”

      Your ad puts emerging AI capabilities on the same line as the council website. That’s probably where most residents will meet AI first. The first questions I’d look at are the ones residents already bring to the council.

      How to check itThe opening of my capability statement, and section 03, under What residents actually ask.
