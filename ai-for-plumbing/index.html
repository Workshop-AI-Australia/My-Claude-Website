AI for plumbing and gas businesses

For plumbing and gas businesses, AI helps most with the calls you can't answer, the quotes you write at night, and the certificates and test reports that pile up after the job. Licensed work and sign-offs stay with the licensed plumber.

Written by Justin Watts, Director of Workshop AI, Hobsons Bay, Melbourne · Last reviewed 15 October 2026
General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Where the time goesFive office jobs AI handles well in plumbing

  • Missed and after-hours calls. An AI assistant can take the caller's name, address, the problem and how urgent it is, then send it to whoever is on call. Burst pipes and suspected gas leaks are set up to go straight to a person.
  • Quotes from photos and voice notes. A photo of a failed hot water unit or a voice memo from a blocked drain becomes a clear quote description with your standard inclusions and exclusions. Prices come from your price book, checked by a person.
  • Rental gas safety checks. Victorian rental providers must have a gas safety check every two years by a gasfitter licensed or registered for Type A appliance servicing. For plumbers who do this work, AI can manage the cycle: due dates, booking access with tenants and property managers, and sending the report.
  • Backflow testing. Testable backflow devices at the meter must be tested every 12 months, with results reported on the water corporation's form, and a failed device must be repaired and retested within 20 business days. AI can track due dates, pre-fill the forms from test data and flag failures so the retest is booked.
  • Chasing quotes. Hot water and drainage quotes often go quiet while the customer shops around. AI drafts follow-ups in your voice and keeps a list of who needs a call, so fewer quotes stall for want of a reminder.

Compliance certificatesWhat AI can do, and what stays with the licence holder

In Victoria, a compliance certificate is required for any plumbing job over $750, counting materials, labour and GST. It's required regardless of value for Type A gas appliance installation, relocation or replacement, fuel conversions, consumer gas piping, below-ground sanitary drains and cooling tower work.

Only a licensed plumber can issue one. The customer must get it within five days of the work being completed, and it must be lodged with the Building and Plumbing Commission within five days, through VBA360. Certificates bought from 22 September 2025 expire 12 months after purchase.

That's a lot of five-day deadlines. AI can check every completed job against the rules, list the certificates due each day, pre-fill them from the job record, chase technicians for missing details, and flag unused certificates before they expire. The licensed plumber reviews, signs and lodges. More complex Type B gas work needs Energy Safe Victoria's acceptance before gas is supplied, and AI can assemble the paperwork for that too.

What's changingGas hot water questions are coming

The Victorian Government says that from 1 March 2027, gas water heaters in existing homes will need to be replaced with electric ones at the end of their life, with some exemptions. Customers will ask what that means for them.

AI can help you answer consistently: drafting clear quote options for electric replacements, a short explainer in your own words, and replies to the questions you hear every week. Check the current rules and rebates before anything goes out, because the details and exemptions are set by government and can change.

A sensible first projectA compliance certificate tracker

Every completed job is checked overnight against the certificate rules. Each morning the office gets a short list: certificates due, with the deadline, a pre-filled draft for each, and any job missing details the plumber needs to supply.

It's narrow, it uses data you already have, and it reduces a deadline risk. Once it works, the same pattern extends to backflow due dates and rental gas safety checks.

5 questionsQuestions we hear

Can an AI receptionist handle emergency plumbing calls?

It can take the details and route the call, but emergencies should reach a person straight away. Set it up so a burst pipe, flooding or a suspected gas leak is put through or escalated immediately, never queued.

Tell callers they're speaking with an AI assistant. The OAIC recommends clearly identifying public-facing AI tools as AI.

Can AI fill in a plumbing compliance certificate?

It can pre-fill one from the job record and check nothing is missing, which is most of the typing. The licensed plumber must check, issue and lodge it, within the five-day timeframes.

Can AI help with backflow test reports?

Yes. AI can keep the register of devices and due dates, pre-fill each water corporation's form from the tester's results, and flag any failed device so the repair and retest happen within the 20 business days allowed. The tester and the licensed plumber still sign.

How should we handle customers asking about replacing gas hot water?

Give them a consistent, accurate answer. AI can draft your quote options and a plain-English explainer, but check it against the government's current rules and rebate information before sending, and update it when they change.
