Tradify and AI

Using AI with Tradify

Partly. Tradify has its own AI, called SmartTools, but we haven't found a public API, a Zapier app or an MCP connector for it, so tools such as Claude and ChatGPT can't plug straight in the way they can with some other job software. There are still useful routes in, through your accounting software, your inbox and Tradify's own exports. Here's a plain guide to what works today.

Written by Justin Watts, Director of Workshop AI, Hobsons Bay, Melbourne · Last reviewed 20 November 2026
General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Four routesHow AI can work with Tradify today

1. Tradify's own AI: SmartTools

Tradify's SmartTools are two features. SmartRead pulls the details off a photo of a supplier bill, so costs land on the job without retyping. SmartWrite fixes spelling, grammar and formatting, writes descriptions from quote and invoice line items, and offers a friendly or professional tone.

Good for: costs and customer-facing wording inside Tradify. Limit: they don't answer questions about the business or work across your other systems.

2. Through your accounting software

Tradify connects to Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks Online and Sage, and its Xero connection shares invoices in both directions. Xero publishes an official MCP server, the standard way AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT use other software, so once invoices reach Xero, AI can read them there.

Good for: money questions, such as who owes what and which invoices are overdue. Limit: Xero doesn't hold your schedule, job notes or quotes still in progress.

3. Through your inbox

Much of what happens around a Tradify job passes through email: customer enquiries, supplier bills and quote follow-ups. An AI assistant connected to your inbox can sort enquiries, draft replies and pull out bills to snap with SmartRead.

Good for: the admin around Tradify. Limit: someone still enters or checks things in Tradify itself.

4. Through exports

Tradify can download reports and exports as CSV files. Drop an export into Claude or ChatGPT and ask questions of it.

Good for: monthly reviews and one-off questions. Limit: it's a snapshot, not live, and someone has to run the export.

Once you're set upQuestions worth asking

  • Through Xero: "Which invoices are more than 30 days overdue, and who should I call first?"
  • From an export: "Which customers have we done the most work for this year, and which have gone quiet?"
  • In your inbox: "Which enquiries from this week haven't had a reply yet?"
  • In your inbox: "Draft a polite follow-up for each quote I sent more than a fortnight ago, for me to check before anything is sent."

Start safelyKeep each system the source of truth for its own data

  • Start read-only. Xero's MCP server can create and update invoices and contacts as well as read them. Choose read-only permissions where the connection allows, and add write access one action at a time.
  • Watch the Xero sync. Logging back in to Xero from Tradify re-imports customers, suppliers and price list items, and can overwrite details stored only in Tradify. If AI edits contacts in Xero, those edits can flow back into Tradify.
  • Treat exports like customer lists. A CSV export holds customer details. Use AI tools in line with the OAIC's guidance on commercially available AI products, and delete files you no longer need.
  • Keep a person on the trigger. Anything sent to a customer and any price should have a person approving it until the results have earned trust.

5 questionsQuestions we hear

Can Claude or ChatGPT connect directly to Tradify?

Not directly, as far as we can find in November 2026. We haven't found a public API, a Zapier app or an MCP connector for Tradify. The practical routes are through Xero or another accounting connection, your inbox and CSV exports.

Does Tradify have an API?

Not a public one that we've found. Tradify connects to accounting software instead. It's part of The Access Group, which agreed to acquire it in October 2024. We'll update this page if a public API arrives.

What do Tradify's SmartTools do?

Two things. SmartRead captures costs from a photo of a supplier bill, and SmartWrite writes and tidies customer-facing text, including descriptions built from quote and invoice line items.

Should we switch job software to get better AI?

Not for AI alone. Switching job software is a big project, and the routes above cover a lot. If you're already weighing a move for other reasons, add how each option connects to AI to your list. ServiceM8, for example, runs its own connector for Claude and ChatGPT: see using AI with ServiceM8.

What does it cost to use AI with Tradify?

SmartTools come with Tradify, so check which of its plans include them. The inbox route needs an AI tool connected to your email.
