Privacy

What we collect when you visit this site or get in touch, why, and what you can do about it. Plain English, kept short.

Who we areWorkshop AI

This site is run by Workshop AI Pty Ltd (ABN 63 698 704 915), based in Hobsons Bay, Victoria. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Questions about privacy go to hello@workshopai.com.au.

What we collectOnly what we need

  • When you visit the site: we use Google Analytics to see how the site is used, such as which pages are read, where visitors come from and which buttons are clicked. It uses cookies and records things like your approximate location, device and browser. It doesn't tell us who you are.
  • When you contact us: your name, email, phone number, business details and whatever you tell us, whether by email, phone or a booking through our calendar page.
  • When we meet: if we meet by video, we may use a note-taking tool to record and transcribe the call. We'll tell you at the start, and you can say no.
  • Business contact details: we sometimes contact businesses using publicly available business contact details, such as those on a company website or a job ad. Ask us to stop at any time and we will.

The site has no forms or accounts, and we don't sell personal information.

How we use itTo reply, to do the work, to improve the site

We use what you give us to respond to you, to scope and deliver work, and to keep records of our conversations. We use the analytics to understand which pages help people and to improve the site. If you become a client, we'll agree how your business information is handled as part of the engagement.

AI toolsWe use AI in our own work

We use AI tools, including Claude, to help with our own admin, such as drafting replies and summarising notes. We use business accounts whose terms say our data isn't used to train their models, and we don't put sensitive information into AI tools.

Who sees itThe services that run the business

We share information only with the services we use to run the business, such as Google (Analytics, email, calendar and the web fonts on this site), GitHub (which hosts this site and logs visitor IP addresses for security), and our note-taking and AI tools. Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Your choicesOpt out, access, correct, complain

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@workshopai.com.au.
  • If you're not happy with how we've handled a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

We'll update this page if anything changes, and the date at the top will show when.
